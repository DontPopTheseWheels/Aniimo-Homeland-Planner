Step 1

🏭 Set Up First — RV, Facilities & Modules

Work from the top down: this step comes first, because what you can make is decided by these numbers. Pick your RV and the boxes fill to the most that RV allows; type over any box if yours differ. Every box starts at 0, so nothing counts as makeable until this step is set — then pick your recipes in Step 2, right below.

🏭 Your facilities & modules

Picking your RV refills every box in this step to the most that RV allows, so the recipe lists in Step 2 match your RV straight away. Type over any box afterwards if yours differ — everything below uses the boxes as they stand.

Picking your RV above, or tapping Fill here, sets every count and level to the most your RV level allows. Type your own numbers in any box afterwards if yours differ — they stand until you pick a different RV or tap Fill again. Levels unlock recipes.

🌡️ Your climate buildings

Counts fill from your RV level too, or type your own (Aniiland roadmap ladder; RV 7 unlock also backed by GameWith). The layout never uses more zones or plots than these counts, only puts out the buildings the plan needs — for every building, not just these: plots needing the same climate building share its zones, Farmland and Woodland together where they fit in the 18 × 18, so owning two of a building never puts both out on its own — the crew staffs only buildings the layout puts out. Steps rest on Aniiland alone, so each count carries the single-source flag.

🧩 Your module levels

Fill sets these too, or type each module's level as shown in Component Upgrade. The planner switches on the matching verified harvest yields. Unverified unlocks stay manual and are never guessed.

🧩 Module harvests in your plan

Strawberry, Coconut and Maple Syrup follow your Ecological level. Premium Wheat has no verified module unlock, so it stays a manual tap, flagged. Without it, Premium Wheat cannot be grown.

Step 2

🧺 Your Recipe Basket

Add up to ten recipes. Ingredients merge into one list, chains included. The lists read your Step 1 numbers above, so what you can make is already settled before you pick. Picks clear when you close the page.

🏭 Facilities not set yet — every box is 0, so nothing counts as makeable yet

Marks in recipe names: ⭐ a special recipe needing a rare-currency unlock.

On: locked recipes leave the lists. Locked means the recipe itself, or anything it needs, is above your facility levels — or it needs Premium Wheat you haven't marked as owned. Picked recipes stay picked either way.

🎒 Your ten recipe slots

One slot per recipe, up to ten. Lists are alphabetical; search and chips narrow all ten at once. To undo a pick, choose "✕ Clear this slot" in its slot.

No amounts to type. The planner sizes production to your facilities, plots and Aniimo limit, and reports per-hour rates below.

🌱 Grow times: should you worry? Not really

Almost everything is ready within about 40 minutes (Wheat 4 min; Potato and Willow Wood 10 min 40 s). Crops grow side by side, so times don't add up — the slowest crop sets the pace. Follow the amounts; grow times rarely matter.

    Step 5

    🗺️ Homeland Layout Helper

    Two layouts, toggled: ⭐ Best plants the full field your RV level allows; 🪶 Minimum plants one plot of each crop. Plots needing the same climate building share its zones — Farmland and Woodland plots together in one zone where they fit — so a plan only ever puts out as many buildings as its plots actually fill, and sharing cuts the Aniimo the crew needs. Sizes: Farmland plot 4 × 4, Woodland plot 7 × 7, one climate building covers 18 × 18.

    🌡️ Climate rates, now used in the layout: temperatures are steps (Freeze, Cool, plain, Warm, Scorching). A crop grows at 100% at its own step, 80% one step off, 50% two steps off and 20% further off single source: Aniimax. The layout still gives every crop its own temperature first; only where crops share a zone set to another temperature does the lower rate show, in plain words beside the crop.

    ⏰ While you're away

    Crops keep going on their own as long as seeds last. Type how many hours you'll be away, and this lists the seeds to have ready for the layout above. Nothing to work out by hand.

    Leave it blank and no list shows.

    ✋ Add extra crops by hand (optional, never needed for layout help)

    Optional extras only — your layout already comes from your basket and strategy plan. Tap a crop to add one plot, tap again to remove it. Hand picks stay in their own group.

    ⚡ Power zones, for later

    Power isn't needed to grow anything — it matters later, when stations switch to E-mode and must sit in a power zone. Generator Aniimo stay at their post: utility posts never count toward Hauling cover.

    🐾 🐾 🐾
    Step 6

    👥 Team Helper

    One crew for the whole plan: your basket plus your strategy plan, deduped by facility, plus the bill's jobs in Level Up mode. ⭐ Best = a dedicated worker per facility; 🪶 Minimum = one shared Aniimo per job, with a second worker only when the first worker's combined workload passes 80%. Best is fitted inside your Aniimo limit — shared workers are named when the limit forces them. Hauling is always in the total, always more than one — the count is your stand-in rule, not game data. Cover, in one line: a worker confirmed to also hold Hauling can carry what it made to storage and counts once, in its main job; everyone else doesn't cover, and the rest becomes dedicated haulers. Utility posts never cover: Aniimo on the Crackle Generator, Cooling Units, Heat Furnaces and Sunlamps can't leave their posts. Aniimo staff only the buildings the plan actually uses — climate buildings only where your layout puts them out, and every other named facility only for the copies the plan's own work needs, never every copy you own. Slots the crew doesn't use become home residents (🏠 below). Crew is shared by ability, not tied to one building: one Aniimo with the right ability can cover every facility that needs that ability at the same time, as long as its workload allows — one Wind Aniimo can cover both Carousel Mills and the Joy Wheel Loom. Minimum shows that sharing in full; Best only adds dedicated workers where speed is worth it. Fields get no personality bonus: Farmland and Woodland planting (Grass) speeds up with the worker's Grass Home Ability level only — no personality match helps it. Other facilities keep their verified matches (the Mine keeps Playful +20%).

    Pick your strategy before you use this crew. In Level Up mode the next level's bill jobs join this crew, so the crew can grow in that mode; in Goals mode the bill plays no part. Switching strategy rebuilds this crew with no leftover jobs from the other mode.

    🐾 🐾 🐾
    Step 7

    🏠 Home & Bud Tickets

    Bud Tickets come from Aniimo living in the Homebuilding Zone (opens at RV 8), spent on decor at the Furniture Store. Production and home share one Aniimo pool: places left for home = cap − your production crew. No reserve number to pick — it moves with the plan.

    Rates follow the Aniimo's tags, not abilities. Places left for home depend only on your plan.

    🎫 How Bud Tickets work

    Per resident per day: 120 regular, 180 Sparkling or Prismana, 270 both tags (Game8). Decor gives about 1 Habitability per ticket, and Habitability gates RV 10–20 (13,000 rising to 20,000, AniimoTools). Level-up rewards and Home Orders add more, not counted here. Not verified: the Jar's capacity (no cap assumed), and a claimed +100/day from a daily home chest (single source, not counted).

    🛋️ Not the same thing as the Leisure buildings

    The Leisure buildings are Production Zone facilities, already staffed in the crew. This section counts residents in the Homebuilding Zone — they need no ability to earn tickets.
