Stage 1

🧺 Your Recipe Basket

Add up to ten recipes. Shared ingredients merge into one list, and crafted ingredients break down into the raw crops and gathered materials under them, so you never chase a chain by hand. Picks live on this page only and clear when you close it.

Sets your facilities: their counts and levels in the Stage 3 panel below always match this level, and recipes above them are greyed out.

Event marks: 🌕 Harvest Moon Festival = season recipe: its Recipe Note comes from the Season Exchange or as a festival quest reward, and each card says which. 🎪 Grand Feast = a dish Jojo asks for at the festival feast (the three season dishes wear both badges). The 🎪 Event & Feast filter above gathers them all in one place. In the recipe lists below, the same emoji sit right in the name: 🌕 for a season recipe, 🎪 for a feast dish, and ⭐ for a special recipe that takes a rare currency unlock (Rose Shortbread, Potato Kvass).

Off: locked recipes stay in the lists with a 🔒 so you can plan ahead. On: locked recipes leave the lists. A recipe already picked stays picked, with its lock note.

🎒 Your ten recipe slots

One slot per recipe, up to ten, matching the basket cap. Every list is alphabetical, and the search box and category chips above narrow all ten lists at once. Picked the wrong recipe? Open its slot and choose "✕ Clear this slot".

Quantities are optional, never required. Every recipe starts at 1, and you can leave them all at 1: just pick what you want to make and the planner works from your picks, basket plan, Layout Helper and crew alike. Raise a quantity only when you need an exact amount for a target, like a Daily Order asking for 3 of something.

🏭 Stage 3 · Your facilities & modules

Every facility below already sits at the most your RV level allows, count and level together, and the list updates on its own whenever you change the RV picker in Stage 1. Recipe greying follows these levels, and the Best Plan below sizes itself from these counts, one batch per processing facility per cycle and one plot per Farmland or Woodland count. 🌡️ Your climate buildings

Counts only, no levels, and nothing to type: like the facilities above, each climate building count below is the most your RV level allows, worked out from the Aniiland RV roadmap ladder (the RV 7 unlock is also backed by GameWith), and it updates on its own whenever you change the RV picker in Stage 1. The planner assumes you put out everything you are allowed. The Layout Helper's layout works with these counts: it never lays out more zones of a building than the count shown here, and never plants more plots than your Farmland and Woodland counts above. At RV 14 all three counts are 2, matching a real RV 14 setup. The ladder steps rest on Aniiland alone, so each count carries the count single source flag, like the other facility counts.

🧩 Your module levels

Type each RV module's level, exactly as the game shows it in Component Upgrade. Every box starts at 0. No need to remember which module recipe you own: the planner checks your levels against the verified unlock table below and switches the matching harvest yields on in the Grow list, the Money Crops table and the Layout Helper plot counts. Only unlocks verified against AniimoTools, aniimoguide and the Aniiland roadmap are automatic. Anything unverified stays manual, says so, and is never guessed.

🧩 Module harvests in your plan

Strawberry, Coconut and Maple Syrup are set by your Ecological level above, so their buttons are locked to match. Premium Wheat has no verified module unlock in any source checked, so it stays a manual tap, plainly flagged, never auto-applied. Without it, Premium Wheat cannot be grown at all.

🌱 Grow times: should you worry? Not really

Good news: almost everything grows on the same 40-minute cycle, so no slow crop hides in your basket. Wheat is the fast exception at 4 minutes, and Potato and Willow Wood take 10 min 40 s. Everything else, Farmland and Woodland alike, is 40 minutes. Crops grow side by side at the same time, so times do not add up: one batch of each crop, planted together, is ready in about 40 minutes total. Time only adds up when one crop needs several batches and the same plots take turns. Check the Plots (batches) column in the Grow list: 1 means one 40-minute cycle, 3 means up to three cycles. Happy to plant, wander off, and come back later? Ignore grow times and just follow the amounts. The amounts are what this planner is careful about.
