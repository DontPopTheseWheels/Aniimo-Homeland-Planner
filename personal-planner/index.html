📌 Always staffed

Pin facilities that must always have a worker, even when no recipe uses them. They are staffed first, before your basket, with the best worker for each facility's job. They count toward your Aniimo limit, and the Always list is never trimmed: if the total goes over your limit, the plan side is trimmed instead.

Always crew: 0 · Plan crew: not set yet · Total: 0

Any staffable facility you own can be pinned. Storage Units take no worker, so they cannot be pinned. Climate units — Cooling Unit, Heat Furnace and Sunlamp — cannot be pinned either: they are never on this list, and only get a worker when a plan's layout actually puts one out.

Use − and + to change a pin, down to 0 and up to the copies you own above. A pin at 0 staffs nobody but stays on your list. If a recipe is picked for a pinned copy, that copy follows the recipe's family rules, like any other copy. A pinned copy with no recipe just needs a worker with the right job.

    Step 5

    ➕ Add & Adjust

    ⭐ Best plants the full field your RV allows. 🪶 Minimum plants one plot of each crop.
    Plots needing the same climate building share its zones, so only the buildings the plots fill go out — never more than your Step 1 counts. Sizes: Farmland plot 4 × 4, Woodland plot 7 × 7, one climate building covers 18 × 18.
    Touch rule, and no overlaps: a plot counts as covered when any part of it touches the zone, so edge plots can hang over the zone's edge and still count in full — and the climate building takes up space too. The building stands in the middle of its zone on its own tiles, so no plot is ever placed on them and no two plots overlap each other — plots pack around the building, and one zone holds up to 25 Farmland or 9 Woodland plots on their own (24 Farmland around a Cooling Unit, whose wider 2 × 2 footprint takes the middle spot), fewer when the two lands share a zone. Keep edge plots clear of other climate buildings, so nothing blends by accident.

    🎛️ Where to find each job pick

    Each pick sits with its facility.
    ⛏️ Mines: pick each Mine's material in the Mines picker in Facilities out below — the plan's own Mines are already set there, and extra Mines you add get their picker there too.

    ➕ Any spare facility: add it in Add a facility this plan isn't using; its pick is on its new block.
    Picks list only what the facility can make at your RV and level.

    Step 6

    👥 Team Helper

    One crew for the whole plan — basket plus strategy, once per facility. In Level Up mode the bill's jobs join too.
    ⭐ Best: one worker per facility. 🪶 Minimum: one shared Aniimo per job; a second joins only when the first passes 80% load. Best is fitted inside your Aniimo limit; forced sharing is named.
    Hauling is always in the total (your stand-in rule, not game data): the plan needs hauling power — 2, plus 1 for every 10 buildings (copies) out, capped at 8 — and the Hauling line shows its math. A worker verified to also hold Hauling covers part of that power, weighted by Hauling level (Lv 4 counts 2, Lv 3 counts 1.5, Lv 1–2 count 1), carries its own output to storage and counts once, in its main job; everyone else doesn't cover, and every fraction rounds up to one more dedicated hauler. Utility posts never cover — Generator, Cooling Unit, Heat Furnace and Sunlamp Aniimo stay at their posts.
    The crew staffs only the buildings the plan uses, at the copies it needs. Unused slots become home residents (🏠 below). Crew is shared by skill, not tied to one building.
    Climate machines: the level of the Aniimo running a Cooling Unit, Heat Furnace or Sunlamp does not matter — any Aniimo with its ability runs it, so those posts read "any level" — the Aniimo named there is picked from your roster like every other post, and any of yours with the ability would do.
    Fields get no personality bonus: planting speeds up with Grass Home Ability level only. The Mine keeps Playful +20%.
    Family-only slots: a gated recipe's crew slot names its family, like "Leisure worker, Susuta family ONLY", never a generic worker.
    Storage Units take no worker: they are passive, with no crew slot, no crew count and no worker-based power math.
    📌 Always staffed: your Step 1 pins are staffed first, stand first in the table below, marked Always, and count toward your limit. If the total goes over, the plan side is trimmed; the Always list never is.
    🌱 Land Cultivation: one Earth Aniimo always stands on land duty — cultivating and reclaiming land, Earth's other job in the game's own Home Abilities text — as a standing post of its own, separate from the Mine posts. It is picked after the facility and Mine posts, from your best Earth Aniimo still free, and counts toward your limit like every other post.

    Your crew picks come from your roster as of Nov 22, 2026 — 179 of your 212 home Aniimo are pickable (battle party, Release-marked and undecided are never picked). A post whose asked level nobody free meets is stepped down to your best Aniimo holding that job at any level, labelled on its row; only a job nobody you own holds at any level stays unfilled.

    🏰 Family rules (Sparkle's): which family staffs a leisure station depends on what the station is producing — every family tag in the planner's data is enforced. A Tidewhisper Sandcastle making Sea Salt goes to the Susuta family first; making Pearls, to the Shelly family first; making Sweet Cone, to the Sugar family first. A Dewy House making Aromathyst goes to the Dewy family first. A Nimbus Bed making Wool — either Wool formula, the Lv 1 one or the Lv 2 Quick Recipe: Wool — goes to the Nimbi family first; making Petals, to the Iris family first. A Starfall Hammock gathering Star goes to the Celestis family first; gathering Gold-Speck Cap, to the Coinwisp family first. A Floral Windmill gathering Scales goes to the Flutternym family first. A family is its whole evolution line — the Dewy family is Dewy and Fragrancier, the Iris family is Iris, Irisal and Irisalis, and so on. Family first means that family ranks ahead of every other Aniimo for that post, ahead of ability level too, and the pick still shows its step-down label when it lands below the asked level. When a Sandcastle post's production is known, that production rule replaces the standing fallback — Panpanta and Shelly families first — which still applies whenever a Sandcastle post has no production assigned; a post at any other station with no production assigned picks by the normal ranking. If a tagged family has no pickable member in your roster at all, the post still fills by the normal ranking and its row says the family has no pickable member you own. Turbo and Gracewing never work the Sandcastle, whatever it is making — they can still be picked for every other post.

    Step 7

    🏠 Home & Bud Tickets

    Bud Tickets come from Aniimo living in the Homebuilding Zone (opens RV 8), spent on decor at the Furniture Store. Work and home share one Aniimo pool: places left for home = cap − your production crew.

    Rates follow the Aniimo's tags, not abilities. Places left for home depend only on your plan.

    🎫 How Bud Tickets work

    Per resident, per day: 120 regular. 180 Sparkling or Prismana. 270 both tags (Game8).
    Decor gives about 1 Habitability per ticket. Habitability gates RV 10 to 20 (13,000 rising to 20,000, AniimoTools).
    Level-up rewards and Home Orders add more. They are not counted here.
    Not checked: the Jar's capacity (no cap is used), and a claimed +100 a day from a daily home chest (single source, not counted).

    🛋️ Not the same thing as the Leisure buildings

    The Leisure buildings sit in the Production Zone. They are already staffed in the crew. This part counts residents in the Homebuilding Zone. Residents need no skill to earn tickets.

    This is your homeland as it stands — on the grid, and always current.     ⚠️ Three rules this layout lives by

    Hauler is load-bearing. Inferlupa (Hauling 4) isn't optional: finished goods pile up at the facility, and when the pile is full, production stops — he carries it to storage. That's why Mine 8 above is unstaffed. Food bowls full = full speed: an empty bowl drops every worker to ×0.2 speed. Workers don't wander to help: when inputs run out, an Aniimo stops at his own facility and never starts another building on his own — moving him along a chain is your job. If a line stalls, check in this order: ① inputs in the warehouse? ② food bowl full? ③ output pile cleared by the hauler?
