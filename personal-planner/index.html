⚡ Power, folded in — the planner decides when it's worth it

From RV 12, a processing machine inside your power grid can switch to E-Mode: it runs on a timer with no Aniimo working it. The Crackle Generator (2 × 2, covers 11 × 11) makes the power — but it doesn't run itself: a Lightning Aniimo works it, and its supply is capped by its level (600 / 800 / 1000 / 1200 / 1500 at Lv 1–5). The Crackle Power Pole (1.5 × 1.5, covers 7 × 7) makes no power at all — it only extends the grid's reach, and it has to overlap an already-powered area. Every E-Mode machine draws power: 15 × its level for most, 30 × level for the Mine, Well, Aniipod Maker and Dance Pad Polisher. While supply stays ahead of demand the grid runs faster, up to 120%; the moment demand passes supply, everything on it slows below 100%.

So the planner only switches a machine when it's efficient: cheap 15 × level processors only — the four 30 × level buildings always stay Aniimo-run — under the fewest generators whose supply still covers the demand, and only when the machines freed outnumber the Lightning Aniimo the generators take. Poles start at zero and go out only for a machine the 11 × 11 can't reach. Your plan's ⚡ Power decision box (in the basket plan and in Step 4) shows the call for the plan you built — which machines, how many generators, the crew it saves — or plainly says skip the grid when it wouldn't pay.

A second building goes out only when the space actually runs out, never just because the two lands differ, and your Step 1 count stays the ceiling, never a demand to fill.     🧭 How this layout is arranged — abilities and zone coverage

    🌡️ Fields follow the crops' climates. The plots are shared out by the climates the planner's crop list actually calls for, and Farmland and Woodland that need the same building pack into the same zone together, largest first: the heat trees (Warm and Scorching) and the Scorching fields ring the three Heat Furnaces; the Freeze and Cool crops ring the three Cooling Units; the Adequate-light crops ring the Sunlamp; and the grows-anywhere crops take the neutral ground in the middle and the room left over in the zones. Every covered plot's center sits inside its 9 × 9 (the center rule), and the climate squares are kept apart, so no two zones blend by accident. The two Starfall Hammocks stand inside the third Cooling Unit's square — the Hammock needs a Cool environment to work — and the Floral Windmill stands inside its own Sunlamp's square, because Scales is hard-gated on Light.

    🐾 Machines cluster by ability, because crew is shared per ability. One Aniimo covers every facility that needs its ability, so the workshop rows group the work: a Fire row (Kilns, Stove, Pots, Claw Cookers), a Wind row (Mills and Looms), then Artisanship (Crafting Tables and Benches), Dark (Dryers and Pickling Jars), Water (Brew Kegs), Perfumery (Phonolfactory Tables), and one Leisure row (Sandcastles, Nimbus Beds, Dewy Houses) — each crew works one short row instead of crossing the map. The 9 Mines stay in one Earth block, the Wells stand by the cold fields their Water crew waters, and the Hatchinators line one row. Storage Units are spread out — one by the Mines, one by the furnace fields, one by the cold fields, one by the light and neutral fields, one by the workshop rows and one by the Hatchinators — because hauling is zone-local: the less an Aniimo walks, the faster things get done.
