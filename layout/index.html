Why this one works

  • Every facility has exactly one home.
  • The workshops stand together in one quarter, so power poles can run down the rows.
  • The mines stay where they already are. Nothing about them changes.
  • The fields and trees never move again. Only seeds and climate units change.
  • Five whole plots stay empty on purpose, so new things get a home without a reshuffle.
  • Whole plots and spare corners stay open on purpose, so there is room to decorate, not just room to work.

What moves

  • The 2 Aniipod Makers come down from the north field corner into Workshop Row 1.
  • The Chimney Kilns, benches and kegs gather from two separate areas into the workshop quarter.
  • Storage Units 6 in total, one each in Plots 14, 15, 16, 7, 12 and 11, spread through the whole layout.
  • Generators and Power Poles stay in her pocket until needed, then drop anywhere the grid needs them.
  • The 2 Nimbus Beds and the leisure stations gather into the Leisure Corner.
  • The Hatchery goes in the empty plot by the Event Plot.

Climates only cover a certain amount of space, so the farm is built out of zones that fit inside one unit's reach. Nothing pretends to cover more than it does.

How a climate change works

  1. Drop a unit on a pad and pick its mode. Heat Furnace is Warm or Scorching. Cooling Unit is Cool or Freeze. Sunlamp gives sun for Adequate crops like Magic Pumpkin and Memory Lanternfruit.
  2. Done with that climate? Pick the unit back up. The plots are Room temp again.
  3. The fields and trees never move. Only seeds and units change.
  4. A unit only works while staffed. Fire runs a Furnace. Ice runs a Cooling Unit. Light runs a Sunlamp. Any level works.

Build the shape once. Swap the climate, not the farm.
