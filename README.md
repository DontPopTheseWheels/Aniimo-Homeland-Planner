# Aniimo Homeland Planner

A fan-made planner for setting up your Aniimo homeland, built for any player.

**Live planner:** https://dontpopthesewheels.github.io/Aniimo-Homeland-Planner/

## What it does

- **Basket:** pick up to 10 recipes and get combined grow, gather, and craft lists in the right order.
- **Facilities & Modules:** see the facility counts and levels your RV level gives you.
- **RV Level-Up:** the exact cost of your next RV level, with an option to fold those materials into your plan.
- **Best Plan:** rank your goals, like Home Coins first, and the planner fills your remaining capacity for you.
- **Money Crops:** the best earning crops by RV level.
- **Layout Helper:** a visual, per-zone layout for what you are growing, with climate buildings placed for you.
- **Team Helper:** one crew list for the whole plan that never goes over your RV level's Aniimo cap.

## Notes

Season 1 edition. Recipes are cross-checked against the Daily Aniimo recipe book and AniimoTools. Anything that could not be verified is flagged on the page itself, never guessed.

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with the game's developers.
